Quick Attendance

Privacy

Your class lists stay with you.

Quick Attendance has no accounts, no database and no analytics. It was built to work entirely on the device you use it on.

Web app

  • The Excel file you open is read and edited inside your browser. It is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Marks are written back to your file, or saved as a copy you choose to download.

Excel add-in and Google Sheets add-on

  • They read and write only the workbook or spreadsheet you have open, and only while you use the panel.
  • Your data stays inside Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, where the file already lives. Nothing is sent to Forma Studio.

Third parties

The pages load the Geist typeface from Google Fonts. The Excel add-in loads Microsoft's Office JavaScript library from Microsoft, which Excel requires for every add-in. Neither receives your class data.
