Quick Attendance for Excel

An Excel add-in for taking class attendance. Open your attendance sheet, click Take Attendance on the Home tab, and tap Present or Absent for each student. Every mark is written straight into the sheet.

Install

  1. Download manifest.xml.
  2. Excel on the web: open any workbook, then Home → Add-ins → More Add-ins → My Add-ins → Upload My Add-in, and choose the file.
  3. For everyone at once, an IT admin can upload the same file in the Microsoft 365 admin center under Settings → Integrated apps → Upload custom apps.
